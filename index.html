    Pavement Condition Index (PCI)

    The Pavement Condition Index (PCI) rates 0–100 the surface condition of the pavement in accordance with ASTM D6433-20 standards. It measures distresses such as cracking, rutting and potholes to indicate structural integrity, safety and to objectively prioritize maintenance and repair needs.

    PCI indicates:

    • Rate of deterioration of pavement
    • Effectiveness of rehabilitation strategies
    • Percentage damage contributed by each distress
    ℹ️ Note
    Data collected using geo-coded roadway videos as per standards. PCI values calculated at 500m road samples using MicroPAVER™, a tool developed by the US Army Corps of Engineers.
